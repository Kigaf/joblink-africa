Privacy notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This notice explains how JobLink Africa handles information when visitors browse job listings or submit a career-service request. We aim to collect only information needed to operate the website and respond to requests.

Information collected

  • Service request details: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, requested service, target role and country, job advert link, delivery preference and instructions.
  • CV documents: a CV file only if you choose to upload one with your request.
  • Order administration: request status, payment confirmation status, administrator notes and delivery link.
  • Website technical information: hosting or infrastructure providers may process technical logs needed to deliver and protect the website.

How information is used

We use submitted information to review and respond to service requests, communicate about the requested work, prepare and deliver agreed materials, confirm payment, provide customer support, protect the service from misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

CV document security

CV uploads are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. They are not intended to be publicly accessible. Access is restricted to authorised JobLink Africa administrators through authenticated access. Do not upload passwords, PINs, bank details or documents that are not needed for your request. Email and WhatsApp delivery, if you choose either, will involve the relevant communication provider.

Service providers

JobLink Africa uses Supabase for database, authentication and private file storage, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy documentation. Information may be processed in locations outside Kenya depending on provider infrastructure.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell CVs or customer contact details. We do not share your CV with prospective employers as part of this service unless you specifically ask us to do so. Information may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the service, customers or others.

Retention and deletion

We keep request information and uploaded CVs only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, provide the service, resolve disputes and meet legitimate record-keeping requirements. You may request deletion of an uploaded CV or service request information. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it or where a legitimate dispute or transaction record must be maintained. Deletion requests are handled by an administrator; the current system does not automatically delete old uploads.

Your choices

You may choose not to attach a CV when submitting a request. You may also ask us to correct inaccurate contact details or request deletion of information, subject to applicable legal requirements. Do not send sensitive account credentials through the service form.

Children

Career services are intended for adults seeking employment. Do not submit another person's CV or personal information without their permission.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when website features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the contact method published on the JobLink Africa website. The site owner should replace any placeholder contact details with a monitored business email before publicly promoting the service.
